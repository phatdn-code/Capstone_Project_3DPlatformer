
# ***Hopix***

## A cute 3D platformer adventure across floating islands, light puzzles, and quirky robotic boss battles.

Jump, explore, and discover colorful sky worlds in a bright and playful journey.

## About the Game

- **Hopix** is a cute 3D platformer set across bright floating islands and imaginative challenge stages.

- Run, jump, explore charming locations, solve light puzzles, and take on robotic enemies in a colorful skybound adventure.

- This project focuses on movement, exploration, and fun boss encounters, all wrapped in a cheerful 3D world.

## Features

- ✨ Cute 3D platforming with bright and playful visuals
- 🌿 Floating islands and colorful worlds to explore
- 🧩 Light puzzles and exploration woven into each area
- ⚙️ Boss battles with unique mechanics
- 🎮 Cheerful adventure atmosphere with movement-focused gameplay

## Worlds

| **World** | *Description* |
|-------|-------------|
| 🌤️ **Greencliff Isles** | *Bright floating islands with peaceful nature vibes* |
| 🏡 **Village Areas** | *Home zones, NPCs, and calmer exploration* |
| 🧱 **Challenge Stages** | *Rails, jumps, and movement-based platforming* |
| 🤖 **Boss Arenas** | *Intense action and mechanical enemies* |

## Boss Battles

Face dangerous robotic bosses that test your movement, timing, and awareness. Each fight is designed to feel playful, intense, and rewarding.

## Why Play Hopix?

- 💛 Easy to pick up  
- 🚀 Fun to move around |
- 🌈 Bright and colorful presentation 
- ⚔️ Memorable boss encounters |
- 🔍 Small discoveries and exploration moments
